Privacy Policy

GemStock is a free inventory app for keeping track of jewelry supplies, finished pieces and designs. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

The short version

GemStock has no accounts and no servers of its own. What you enter stays on your device. We don’t collect, sell or share your information, and the app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What GemStock keeps on your device

Your items and their photos, categories, designs and their costs, suppliers and their contact details, purchase orders, and the business details you enter in Settings. All of it is stored in the app’s private storage on this device.

AI keys you add in Settings are kept in the device’s secure storage (the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). In the web version they are kept in the browser’s storage.

Deleting the app deletes this information. Export a CSV from Import & export if you want a copy.

Camera and photos

GemStock uses the camera only when you take a photo of an item or scan a QR code or barcode, and your photo library only when you pick photos to add. Photos are saved inside the app and are not uploaded anywhere unless you use photo auto-fill.

Photo auto-fill (optional)

Auto-fill is off until you add your own key for Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI) or Gemini (Google) in Settings.

When it is on and you add an item from a photo, GemStock sends a smaller copy of that photo and your list of category names directly from your device to the service you chose, using your key. It does not pass through us. That service’s own privacy policy and terms apply, and any charges are billed to your account with them.

Avoid photographing anything you wouldn’t want to share with that service, such as papers with personal information.

When you share something

When you call, text or email a supplier, export a CSV, or print or share labels, GemStock hands that information to the app you pick — Phone, Messages, Mail, Files, a printer and so on. What happens next is up to that app.

Children

GemStock is a business tool and isn’t directed at children under 13.

Disclaimer

Check auto-fill suggestions

AI can misread a photo. Check the name, category, materials and size it suggests before relying on them.

Numbers are estimates

Costs, sell prices, profit and restock suggestions are worked out from the numbers you enter. They are a guide, not accounting, tax or financial advice.

Keep your own backups

Your inventory lives only on your device, so it can’t be recovered from a lost, broken or reset device. Export a CSV regularly.

Labels

Print a test page before printing a full sheet, since printers and label stock vary.

Provided as is

GemStock is provided “as is”, without warranties of any kind. To the extent the law allows, the makers of GemStock aren’t liable for losses from using the app, including lost data or decisions made from its numbers.
